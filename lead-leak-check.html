The Lead Leak Check

A 10-point self-audit for small builders and brokerages across India — read it in five minutes, score yourself honestly.

Most enquiries do not go cold because the ad failed. They go cold because the callback never happened.

Portal enquiries, hoarding calls and ad leads arrive faster than two or three telecallers can call back. The desk picks the easy ones first, the rest go quiet, and the buyer signs elsewhere. This check finds the leak in your desk — one line at a time.

How to score

For each line, tick it only if it is true on a normal working day. Yes = 1. Not yet = 0. Add your ten marks at the end.
