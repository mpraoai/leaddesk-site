The Farmland Lead Leak Check

A 10 point self audit for land dealers and farmland sellers across India. Read it in five minutes, score yourself honestly.

Land does not go cold because the ad failed. It goes cold because nobody called the buyer back.

Farmland enquiries come from YouTube, Instagram, ads and walk ins. A buyer walks the plot, says he will tell you, and six months later nothing has moved. The plot sits, the farmer waits, and your money stays locked. This check finds the leak in your desk, one line at a time.

How to score

For each line, tick it only if it is true on a normal working day. Yes = 1. Not yet = 0. Add your ten marks at the end.

My score:  0 / 10
01

Every enquiry from YouTube, Instagram, ads and walk ins lands in one place.

Fix today: choose one list and make it the only place an enquiry lives.

02

Every new enquiry gets a reply or a call the same day.

Fix today: put one person on replies, even if you are out on site visits.

03

Every buyer who visited a plot is written down with the date.

Fix today: start a visit log today, name, plot, date and what he said.

04

You know this month's enquiry count and how many became site visits.

Fix today: write both numbers down at the end of the day.

05

Buyers who went quiet are called again at day 7, day 30 and day 90.

Fix today: set those three dates the moment a buyer visits a plot.

06

Every plot has its papers checked before you show it.

Fix today: one checklist per plot, patta, EC, survey number, mutation, boundaries.

07

You can see which plot has been unsold the longest, and why.

Fix today: add two columns, days listed, and the reason it is stuck.

08

Nothing about a buyer sits only in a telecaller's head or a WhatsApp chat.

Fix today: if it is not on the board, treat it as not done.

09

The morning starts from the list, not from missed calls.

Fix today: first twenty minutes, call the oldest waiting buyer first.

10

You know your real conversion, visitors against registrations.

Fix today: count it once a month, one line, so you stop guessing.

8–10 Your desk is holding. Tighten lines 3 and 5, and update the visit log every evening.
4–7 Buyers are going quiet on you. Fix lines 1, 3 and 5 this week, before you shoot another video.
0–3 You are losing enquiries you paid for. Start lines 1, 2 and 3 today. One list, same day reply, a visit log.
